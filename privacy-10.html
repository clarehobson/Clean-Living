🌿Clean Living

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Clean Living is a recovery app for people in the 12 step fellowship. We know the trust it takes to use an app like this, so we keep what we collect to a minimum and we never sell your data. This page explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Clean Living ("we", "us", "our") is operated by cleanliving.app. If you have any questions about your privacy, you can reach us any time at hello@cleanliving.app.

2. What we collect

Your account

When you sign up we collect your email address and a password (stored securely and never visible to us in plain text). These are used to create and protect your account.

Your profile

Information you choose to add: a first name or username, your country and region, your clean date, an optional profile photo, an optional status, and any social links you decide to share. You control all of this and can edit or remove it at any time.

We also ask your gender when you sign up. It is used to decide which features appear for you: the Perimenopause & HRT tracker is shown automatically to members who choose Female, Non-binary members can switch it on or off themselves, and it is not shown to men.

Your country is also used to show you things that fit where you live, such as the foods, products and portion sizes in the food diary, and nearby events. If we ask you to confirm your country, the app suggests one using your phone’s own region setting; that suggestion is worked out on your phone and nothing else about your device is read.

Your recovery activity

Content you create in the app, such as journal entries, step work, community posts, photographs you share to the community feed, mood check-ins, meeting logs, stillness sessions, service commitments, your sponsorship tree, your progress through fitness programmes, the workouts you finish and build, the events you say you’re attending, and everything you do in the nutrition feature (see below). Your journal entries and step work are private and visible only to you. Community posts, including photographs, are visible to other signed-in members.

Messages

If you message another member, the content of those messages is processed and stored by our chat provider so it can be delivered and shown to you both.

Your phone number

If you choose to add a phone number to your profile, we use it to send you important account and service messages — including updates about the app, your account, and changes that affect your ability to use Clean Living. These are sent by SMS. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; you may still receive essential service messages where the law allows.

How you use the app

We collect basic, app-level usage information (for example, that a feature was opened or a meeting search was run) to understand what's working and improve Clean Living. We do not track your precise location, and we do not build advertising profiles.

While the app is open, it lets us know you're using it. We keep the time you were last active (so members can see who is online) and a simple daily record: which days you used the app, whether on iPhone or Android, the first and last time you were active that day, and roughly how long. This is used only to understand how many people use Clean Living and how often, so we can improve it. It does not include what you write, read or log, and it is never sold or used for advertising. It is removed when you delete your account.

Emails

If you tick the box to receive updates, we may email you about new features and changes to Clean Living, from hello@cleanliving.app. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also change this in your profile at any time. Emails you need about your account itself may still be sent.

Device permissions

  • Notifications — if you allow them, we use a notification token to send you alerts about messages, connection requests, and activity. You can turn these off any time in your device settings.
  • Camera & photos — only used if you choose to add a profile photo, share a daily photograph to the community feed, photograph an event flyer so the app can read its details, photograph a written workout plan so the app can turn it into workouts (the photo is sent to be read and is not stored), or scan a barcode in the food diary. Barcode scanning happens on your phone: the camera picture is not saved or sent anywhere, only the barcode number is used to look the product up. Photographs you share are resized on your device before upload and that resized copy is what we store; location and other metadata held in the original file are not kept, and your original stays on your phone.

Health and fitness data (optional)

If you choose to connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), Clean Living reads your workouts, step count, active energy and sleep so it can show you your own movement in the No matter what tab.

With your permission, Clean Living can also write to Apple Health or Health Connect: finished Clean to 5K sessions are saved as a walk or a run, and food and drink you log in the food diary are saved as nutrition entries (calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre). This is so the things you do in Clean Living count alongside the rest of your activity, in the health app you already use. Writing is entirely optional, asked for separately from reading, and you can decline or withdraw it at any time — the rest of the app carries on working as normal.

Health data we read stays on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers, never stored in our database, and never shared by us. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not passed to any third party. Anything we write goes only into your own Apple Health or Health Connect, on your phone — it is not sent anywhere else.

The one exception is your own choice: if you tap Share my day, a picture of your day card is posted to the community, and that picture can include the day’s step count. It is only ever posted when you tap to share it, and you can delete the post at any time.

You can withdraw access at any time — on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Clean Living, and on Android in the Health Connect app under App permissions. The feature simply stops showing your movement; nothing else changes.

Programmes

Which week and session you have reached in a programme such as Clean to 5K, and a record of each session you complete (its length and whether you finished it), are saved to your account so they survive a new phone. This is your own data, visible only to you.

While a Clean to 5K session is running, the app shows an ongoing notification and keeps a timer going in the background. This is what allows the spoken guidance to carry on when your screen is off and your phone is in your pocket. It stops as soon as the session ends.

Workouts you finish in Clean Strength, Clean Yoga or My workouts (the programme, exercises, sets, reps and weights), and workouts you build yourself, are saved to your account in the same way, so your history and next weights are there on any phone.

Nutrition and the food diary

If you use the nutrition feature, we save to your account: your meal plan, your shopping list and what you’ve ticked off, and your food diary — each thing you log, the meal it was for, the amount, and its calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre — plus your preferences, such as whether to hide calories. This is your own data, visible only to you, and it is removed when you delete your account.

To find foods, the app searches two government food databases held in our own database (the UK’s McCance and Widdowson’s CoFID and the US Department of Agriculture’s FoodData Central), so those searches don’t leave our service. Searching for a branded product, or scanning a barcode, sends the words you typed or the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public product database, so it can return the product. Nothing that identifies you is sent with it.

Perimenopause & HRT tracker (optional, Pro+)

If you choose to use the Perimenopause & HRT tracker, we save to your account what you enter: the medicines you add and their schedules, the doses you mark as taken (including where you put a patch, if you choose to record it), coil or ring dates, and your daily symptoms, periods and notes.

This information is private to you. It is protected so that only your account can read it. It is not shown to other members, it is never shared with the AI Coach, and it is not passed to any third party, used for advertising or sold.

Reminders are scheduled on your own phone. You can choose not to show a medicine’s name on your lock screen. Deleting a medicine removes its history, and deleting your account removes all of your tracker data. The tracker is a personal record: it does not give medical advice, and it never suggests doses.

3. AI features

Clean Living includes optional AI features. When you use any of them, the information needed is passed to Anthropic (the maker of Claude AI), through our secure proxy, only so a reply, reading or estimate can be generated. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to be used to train its models.

  • AI Coach (Pro+) — the messages you send, your first name and clean time, and a summary of your last week in the app: workouts you finished in the app (including lifts), meetings, mood, stillness, reflection days, and how many days you used the food diary. The summary never includes calories, your weight, the content of your journal or step work, any data read from Apple Health or Health Connect, or anything from the Perimenopause & HRT tracker. It is on unless you switch it off at the top of the chat. Your conversation is stored on your phone, not by us.
  • Copy a plan from a photo — the photos you choose, so the plan can be read. We do not store them.
  • Meal estimates — only the description you type and your country (so portion sizes fit where you live).

Our earlier AI companions, Noah and Saj, are no longer offered, and Noah no longer sends check-ins. Any past Noah conversations and check-ins stay in your account until you delete your account or ask us to remove them. Using any AI feature is entirely optional.

The AI Coach is a supportive tool only. It is not a therapist, sponsor, personal trainer or medical professional, and it does not provide crisis support. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a helpline straight away.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide and run the app — your account, profile, journal, community, messages, meetings, programmes and food diary.
  • To personalise your experience, such as your clean time, reflections, and foods suited to your country.
  • To send notifications and emails you've opted into.
  • To understand how many members use the app and subscribe, so we can keep improving it.
  • To keep the community safe and respond to reports of objectionable content.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we never have. We share information only with the trusted service providers that make the app work, and only as needed to provide the service:

  • Apple and Google — handle all subscription payments, through the App Store and Google Play. We never receive or store your card details. On Android, barcode scanning uses Google’s code scanner in Google Play services, which runs on your phone.
  • Supabase — our database and account hosting.
  • Stream — powers direct messaging.
  • Twilio — delivers SMS messages to the phone number you provide.
  • OneSignal — delivers push notifications, and update emails to members who have opted in (your email address and username are held by OneSignal for this).
  • RevenueCat — keeps track of subscription purchases, trials, renewals and cancellations from Apple and Google, linked to your account ID (see section 6).
  • Anthropic — generates AI Coach replies, reads workout plan photos and generates meal estimates (only the information described in section 3, while you use the feature).
  • YMove — provides the exercise videos. We request videos by exercise only; none of your personal information is shared.
  • Cloudflare — provides the secure proxy that routes those AI requests, and caches and delivers photographs shared to the community feed.
  • Open Food Facts — receives product search words and barcode numbers when you look up a product (nothing that identifies you).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our members.

6. Subscriptions

Pro+ (and original Pro) are optional auto-renewing subscriptions, billed through your Apple ID on iPhone or your Google Play account on Android. All payment processing is handled by Apple or Google — we do not see or store your payment information. You can manage or cancel at any time in your Apple ID account settings or your Google Play subscriptions.

To keep track of subscriptions, we use RevenueCat. When you subscribe, start a free trial, renew, cancel or your subscription ends, Apple or Google tells RevenueCat, and RevenueCat passes the details to us: which plan, the store, the dates, the price, and the trial or renewal status. It is linked to your Clean Living account using your account ID (not your name or email). We store this so your plan works correctly and so we can see how many members subscribe. Card details are never included.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Photographs you share to the community feed are kept for as long as the post exists — deleting the post removes the stored image files too. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your profile, journal entries, connections, social events, programme progress, meal plans, food diary, Perimenopause & HRT tracker entries and associated data. This cannot be undone. Anything the app wrote to Apple Health or Health Connect stays there, under your control, until you remove it in that app. Your AI Coach conversation is kept only on your phone, until you clear it in the chat or delete the app.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit and access controls on our database. No method of storage or transmission is ever completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access & correction — view and edit your profile and content at any time in the app, including editing or removing anything in your food diary.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data from your account settings.
  • Notifications — turn push notifications on or off in the app or your device settings.
  • Emails — unsubscribe using the link in any email, or switch updates off in your profile.
  • Camera & photos — decline access and still use the rest of the app, including typing foods in by hand.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect — decline reading, writing, or both, or withdraw either later in your device settings, and use the rest of the app as normal.
  • AI features — ignore them entirely, or switch off the AI Coach’s weekly summary at the top of the chat.

If you'd like help exercising any of these, email us at hello@cleanliving.app.

10. Children

Clean Living is open to all ages, in keeping with the fellowship. If you are under 18, you confirm you have the consent of a parent or guardian to use the app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will let you know through the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email hello@cleanliving.app and we'll be glad to help.
